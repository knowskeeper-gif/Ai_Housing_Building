GIS Hazard Auditor Analysis

Our **Geospatial Information System (GIS)** audits environmental safety parameters to pre-determine structural reinforcement requirements:

  • Flood Lowlands mapping: Evaluates historical rain patterns and storm basin levels to enforce raised column foundations.
  • Seismic Fault auditors: Measures active distance coordinates to phivolcs regional shear fault lines.
  • Landslide assessment: Scans soil moisture indexes and sloped land terrains to check stability.
Geospatial safety is secured: Recommendations dynamically calibrate structural steel cages and footing depths.
